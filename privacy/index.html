English

This Privacy Policy explains how PaperFrog (the “App”) collects, uses, stores, and protects user information. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App can be played without creating an account. Ranking participation is optional and uses a nickname chosen by the user.

Optional Ranking Participation

  • User-chosen nickname
  • App-generated anonymous installation identifier
  • Ranking record data such as score, clear time, jumps, and created time

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect or process:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers such as Advertising ID or App Set ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network data
  • App interaction and ad performance information
  • Cookies or similar technologies where applicable

Locally Stored Gameplay Data

The App stores some gameplay data locally on the device, including:

  • Best score
  • Best clear time
  • Best jump count
  • Heart state and refill timing
  • Ranking nickname
  • Anonymous ranking installation identifier

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide optional ranking and player record features
  • To store and restore gameplay-related progress on the device
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App

3. Data Storage

Ranking-related data may be processed and stored using Google services such as Cloud Firestore.

Gameplay records needed only on the device may be stored locally using device storage.

4. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our policies.

  • Local gameplay data remains on the device until the user deletes the app or clears app data.
  • Optional ranking data may remain until the user clears ranking participation, requests deletion, or the service is discontinued.

5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell user data.

However, data may be processed by third-party service providers as part of the App’s functionality:

  • Google / Firebase for ranking, analytics, and backend services
  • Google AdMob for advertising, fraud prevention, and ad measurement

6. Security

Data transmitted to third-party services is sent using industry-standard security measures such as HTTPS/TLS. We rely on the security controls provided by Google Play Services, Firebase, and related SDK providers.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Deletion and Ranking Requests

Users may stop participating in rankings inside the App by clearing their ranking nickname. Users may also request deletion of ranking-related data by contacting us at twoweeksdad@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

twoweeksdad@gmail.com
